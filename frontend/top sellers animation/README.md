# PowerFuel Top Sellers section

A responsive storefront section inspired by the supplied layout: a continuously looping SHREDTEIN campaign-video showcase sits on the left, and the two supplied product visuals drive the top-seller cards on the right.

## Use it

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve the folder with any static web server.
2. `assets/shredtein-ani-video.mp4` begins muted and loops automatically. Browsers require muted video for reliable autoplay.
3. The layout shows the two products side by side on larger screens and stacks them on mobile. The video and product photography respond with smooth hover zoom effects.

## Customize

- Product names, prices, reviews, direct product routes, and Buy now / Add to cart controls are all in `index.html`. Replace the `/products/...` routes with the matching URLs from your own store before publishing.
- Colors, layout, responsive behaviour, text entrance animation, and card hover animation are in `styles.css`.
- Video replacement, video play/pause, carousel interaction, and checkout/cart feedback are in `script.js`.
- Supplied media is stored locally in `assets/`, so the product visuals and the campaign video travel with the package.

Before publishing, verify prices, claims, reviews, and any trademark permissions against your real store catalogue.
