ℹ️ About Our Verification System

  • We check if the barcode/SKU is registered in the NUTRATEIN product database.
  • Verification confirms the product exists in our catalog — not that your specific unit is authentic.
  • If a barcode is not found, it may be a counterfeit, an older product, or an entry error.
  • For genuine authenticity concerns, contact our support team with your order details.
