OUR STORY & STANDARDS

Science-Backed Fitness Nutrition Without Compromise

PROTEINX is built around a single conviction: Indian lifters and athletes deserve absolute transparency, pure intact proteins, and formulas verified by honest testing.

Our Mission

For years, sports nutrition consumers in India have navigated an uncertain market fraught with amino spiking, hidden sugars, grey-market supply lines, and unverified potency claims.

We founded PROTEINX to eliminate guesswork. Every scoop delivers exact label accuracy, microfiltered intact protein fractions, and bioavailable mineral complexes that fuel real physical adaptations.

The Three Quality Pillars

🔬

Zero Amino Spiking

We never inflate protein ratings with glycine, taurine, or nitrogen fillers. 100% of the listed protein comes from intact dairy and plant isolates.

⚡

Digestive Enzymes

Enriched with DigeZyme® multi-enzyme matrix (protease, amylase, lipase, lactase) ensuring smooth gut transit and rapid bioavailability without bloating.

📦

Direct Authenticity

Products are shipped directly from climate-controlled fulfillment hubs to your home with QR batch authentication, eliminating unauthorized intermediaries.
